↓
REWARD 05

↓

Downregulates autonomic nervous system to allow tissue repair and memory consolidation. Task: Standardize evening wind-down and consistent sleep window.
DAYS 01–07 ACTIVATE
Focus on establishing baseline consistency: morning sunlight exposure, brisk walking, and preparing tomorrow's task outline each evening.
DAYS 08–14 BUILD
Escalate resistance exercises, include targeted stair climbing, and strictly delay selected immediate-reward digital distractions.
DAYS 15–21 CHALLENGE
Complete mandatory deep-work task prior to any entertainment. Maintain structured sleep/recovery rituals consistently.
DAYS 22–30 CONSOLIDATE
Seamlessly integrate physical exertion, impulse friction, and deliberate recovery into an automatic daily operating rhythm.
